Problem
Pivoting careers is difficult. People rarely know what target jobs truly require, where their real skill gaps lie, or how to prepare while juggling dozens of applications.

What I built
I built Tack in one day for the Product Space Agentic AI Hackathon. It is a career pivot coach that compares your resume against live job postings. In tests, it scanned 10,654 listings across 60 company boards, matched 450 AI Product Manager roles, and read 40 in 11 seconds to build a skill-gap map in 12 seconds.

Tack organizes those gaps into a weekly study plan with checkpoints, calendar exports, and three portfolio projects. It drills you with five mock interview questions scored 1 to 5 against a rubric. It also opens Chrome to apply for you. One test application took 17 seconds; three in parallel took 35 to 71 seconds. On real company forms, in dry runs that were never submitted, it filled 8 of 11 fields on Greenhouse, 12 of 21 on Lever, and 6 of 7 on Ashby, attaching the resume every time. An autopilot mode runs every 12 hours, sorts email replies, and uses Thompson sampling to prioritize roles with the best response rates. The project includes 176 passing tests, an 8-tool MCP server, and a macOS desktop app.

How it uses AI
Tack uses Gemini through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The model reads and judges: it extracts skills, drafts project briefs, scores interviews, and sorts replies. Code handles the math, including job rankings, readiness scores, and schedules. Every quote from the model is checked against original text or dropped. Built-in fallbacks keep the system running if the model goes down.

What is different
Career tools often stop at generic advice. Tack proves every matched skill and bullet with exact quotes from source texts. Instead of just offering advice, it applies in a real browser and learns from actual responses.

Honest limits
The demo uses fictional profiles, local forms, and sample emails. Real Gmail integration was tested only to Google's sign-in screen. Live forms leave pay, gender, and relocation fields for you. The macOS app is unsigned, the web preview cannot apply or read mail, and the free Gemini tier has strict limits.

Built on
I reused Fanfold, my earlier browser agent, for board scraping, resume parsing, and browser automation. Everything else was built during the hackathon.
